Hypothesis 1: Germany and USA participants differ in levels of individualism

Variables:

Country raised (Country_raised): a categorical variable defining where participants were raised, in this case only looking at participants who respond with “Germany” or “United States”

Individualism: a composite variable compiled of three separate individualist/collectivist-determining questions (Individualism_col_1, Individualism_col_2, Individualism_col_4) that were reverse-scored.

# making these columns numeric
data[,5:7] <- lapply(data[,5:7], as.numeric)

# creating a total account of individualist questions
data$individualistTotal <- rowSums(data[,5:7],na.rm=FALSE)

# create reversed mean of individualist to measure individualism
data$individualism <- 8 - (rowMeans(data[ , 5:7], na.rm = TRUE))
